01The shop

It’s a Legacy thing.

Legacy Barbershop® blends classic grooming with modern style: sharp, on-trend cuts, soothing hot towel treatments and precise straight razor shaves. Skilled barbers, a shop on NE 4th Ave with art on the walls, and a chair where tradition and new technique meet.

Every visit is more than a haircut. These come with every service on the menu:

  • Hair wash
  • Complimentary drink
  • Grooming consultation
  • Styling

Tradition and innovation, one chair at a time.
